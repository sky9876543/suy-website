THE PRODUCT DIRECTION

Creative tools.
Built for continuity.

For illustrators, animators, and small creative teams who want to carry their ideas and assets from one scene to the next.

The problem

Planning scenes, rebuilding similar assets, and moving between production tools can create repetitive work. We want to make those steps more connected while keeping the artwork editable.

What we are developing

A software toolkit for structured story and scene planning, reusable visual components, and illustration production. Animation workflows are part of the longer-term direction.

How it will reach creators

We intend to sell software or tools to users. Our first stage is internal prototyping and evaluation. Pricing and public availability have not been announced.

INSIDE DEVELOPMENT

A real prototype.
A work in progress.

SUY is a self-funded project based in Vietnam. These are development experiments, not a released product.

IMPLEMENTED PROTOTYPE

Structured story planning

An experimental compiler validates story and shot records and prepares production handoffs. It preserves unresolved requirements for review. It does not render animation.

DEVELOPMENT EXPERIMENT

Editable scene assembly

Experimental tools assemble authored components into static Illustrator scenes. The image above is a retained output from that workflow. Visual review remains part of development.

PLANNED INTEGRATION

Claude-assisted workflows

We plan to use Claude for story and scene planning, asset organization, and structured production instructions. API integration and evaluation are planned development work.

Current focus: internal evaluation, component reuse, and reliable handoffs between creative steps. Public release and pricing are still ahead.

OUR APPROACH
